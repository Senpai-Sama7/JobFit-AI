Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans anytime? +

Yes! You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate any charges.

What happens after my free trial? +

After your 14-day trial, you'll be automatically subscribed to your chosen plan. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends to avoid charges.

Is my data secure? +

Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade encryption, SOC 2 compliance, and never share your data with third parties. Your resumes and personal information are always protected.

Do you offer refunds? +

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not satisfied, contact our support team for a full refund.

How does team billing work? +

Team plans are billed per seat. You can add or remove team members anytime, and billing adjusts automatically based on your team size.
